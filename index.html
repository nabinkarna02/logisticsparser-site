How it works

Three steps, all by email.

1

Send one invoice

Email a commercial invoice or packing list PDF (digital or scanned) to hello@logisticsparser.com. Redact names, prices or anything else you like.

2

We structure it

Header fields and every line item are pulled into rows and columns, keeping HTS numbers exactly as the supplier printed them.

3

Get Excel or JSON back

Within 24 hours you receive the file in the format you prefer. Check it against the original and decide if it saves your team time.

What comes back

The fields brokers usually retype from an invoice before an entry can start.

Header

  • Invoice no. and date
  • Shipper and consignee
  • Incoterms and currency
  • B/L and container
  • Ports of loading / discharge
  • Invoice total

Every line

  • Part number
  • Description
  • HTS as printed
  • Country of origin
  • Quantity and unit
  • Unit price and value
  • Metal content, if stated

Your format

Excel (separate Header and Lines sheets) or JSON. Tell us if you need specific column names or order to match your system.

Privacy

You do not need to send client documents to try this. A redacted invoice works fine: black out names, addresses, prices or anything you prefer not to share. The trial is about whether the layout and line items come through cleanly.

Files you send are used only to produce your output, are not shared with anyone else, and are deleted on request. Just reply and ask.

Logistics Parser does not classify goods, give tariff advice or file anything. HTS numbers are copied as printed on the document. Your licensed team stays in charge of every decision.
